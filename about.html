The long version

How I got here

A ridge, a failed stomach, and a chart I finally agreed to live instead of quote.

A kid with too many questions

I grew up in Grass Valley. I wanted to know how a body decides, how a forest decides, and why adults said one thing at the table and another thing in the car. The answers felt like they were stored in people, not in the lectures.

I started a botany degree at Humboldt. The labs were careful and I respected them. By the second year I was spending more nights on a ridge above Arcata than in the library. I left in 2006. The plants did not seem offended.

Making myself smaller

I am a Generator in a culture that claps for the person who starts things. I tried to be easy. Quiet job, quiet chair, quiet opinion. It did not make me easy to be around. People still felt me walk into a room, and I decided that meant I should walk into fewer of them.

I got good at disappearing inside a life that looked fine from the driveway. Friends thought I was mellow. I was braced.

The stomach

In my early thirties my digestion quit. Food hurt. Doctors ran the tests they had and sent me home with a shrug and a bland diet. I had spent years overriding hunger, sleep, and the wish to leave rooms. My gut kept the minutes.

One night I sat on the cabin floor and stopped bargaining. I told my body I would follow the next true signal even if it made me look strange. The first signal was specific and a little embarrassing. Sleep before midnight. No coffee on an empty stomach. Call my brother back.

It took four years for eating to feel ordinary again. During that stretch I trained in BodyTalk with a practitioner in Sebastopol, started sitting with the Gene Keys because the botany kid in me trusted a system that talked like code, and ran the Human Design experiment as a life instead of a party trick.

What I do with it

I still live outside Occidental. Sessions are by phone. Mentorship is for people who want someone on the line while their week rearranges. I am not interested in making you luminous. I am interested in you telling the truth at dinner.

The design I work from is a 5/1 Generator with emotional authority. I wait for a yes that survives the night. If you need someone who will book you in the same breath as your email, I am the wrong cabin.

Why I bother

A lot of capable people spend their best hours managing a self they invented to survive a job, a family, a town. The original equipment was fine. It is particular, and it does not look like the equipment next door.

I like the moment someone stops apologizing for the particular part and starts using it. That is the job.
