The agreement

Timing matters more than a packed calendar. I wait for a yes that survives the night, and I muscle-check the gap before I offer the next date. The reason for a wait is often obvious only afterward.

The more room you give the process, the more specific the work can be. We both leave the script at the door. Take what resonates. Leave the rest. I will tell you what I am sensing, and you get to say if it is wrong.

How the series runs

After you write, I gather your chart and your Gene Keys profile and let them percolate. The prep starts when you commit, not when we dial.

We book the first session when the notes are ready. After that sitting I ask your body, by the check, when it wants the next one. That window is a guide. My own timing still has to agree, which tends to be the same week you feel the pull to come back.

Each session runs like the single body session. About 75 minutes, by phone.

Each sitting includes

  • Human Design chart, drawn once and reused
  • Gene Keys profile
  • A BodyTalk session
  • A short note on what to practice before we meet again
