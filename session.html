Body session

$480

One sitting for the pattern that keeps rebuilding itself. We use a muscle check to find the piece that wants to move, then we move it. About 75 minutes, by phone.

Bring the real snag. A relationship, a body symptom you have already had checked by a doctor, a decision you keep remaking. We will not sand the edges off it so the hour feels nice.

Write to book

Contemplative bodywork, not medical care.

What we do in the hour

We look for the knot under the story. Thought, belief, mood, chemistry, the old family line. The check sets the order, which is rarely the order you would have picked from the couch.

I narrate as we go. You can disagree. Take what lands and leave what does not. I am a facilitator with a method, not the authority on your life.

If your chart belongs in the room, we use it. If it does not, we leave it in the drawer.

After you write

I ask for birth data and the focus for the session. I draw a Human Design chart and a Gene Keys profile and let them sit until I know what is mine to say.

Then we set a time. I will not book against my own timing. That delay is how the session stays specific.

You leave with the charts for your own study, the shift we made, and one way to practice it before the week swallows it.

You leave with

  • Human Design chart
  • Gene Keys profile
  • A BodyTalk session aimed at the priority your body named
  • Notes on what to watch for in the days after
