What the months hold

  • One Human Design walkthrough at the start, so the rest of the work uses your actual design.
  • One longer call each month for whatever you are in the middle of.
  • One body session each month, led by the muscle check.
  • Voice notes between sessions. A real reply when you are wobbly, not a performance of being available all day.

Same tools as the single sessions. Your chart, the Gene Keys profile when it is useful, and BodyTalk as the floor under both. Your body sets the order.

What you should know before you write

I book on a delay

Emotional authority means I sleep on timing. If I say Thursday, Thursday is the day the work will land. A real yes behind a small action beats a frantic calendar. Sometimes the muscle check asks us to wait, and the reason shows up later.

The month is the container

Calls are by phone. The hour matters, and so does the grocery store afterward. You will get poked by ordinary life. That is on purpose. I stay available while you try the new behavior where it counts.

You keep the skill

Life starts reflecting the room you are actually standing in. That mirror does not expire when the mentorship does. I am not required for the second year.
