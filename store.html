Before you write

How long is a session?

About 75 minutes, give or take. The chart hour is closer to 60.

I sent a note. Now what?

I reply with the next step. Birth info if we need a chart, and a time once I have slept on it.

When will we meet?

New people usually get chart notes within two days, and a meeting time inside the next week. I keep you posted. If we have worked together, we skip a lot of the ramp.

What is it like?

Deep enough to matter, plain enough to follow. I tell you what I am seeing as I see it. Trust is useful. Blind faith is not required.

Payment plans?

Yes for the series. Say which tier you want in the email. The chart hour and the single body session are paid once.

How do I reach you?

Through the contact page, or straight to jack@jackinhoff.com.
